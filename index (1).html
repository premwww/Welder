Crafted with pride & precision: meet Ramesh Sharma

I started Sharma Metal Fabrication with a simple belief: steel should be forged with integrity and welds should outlive the buildings they hold up. For nearly two decades, my team and I have bent, cut, and welded iron, mild steel, and stainless steel for homes, factories, and construction sites across Rajasthan.

We don't take shortcuts. Every weld is inspected for penetration and structural strength, and every custom gate or railing is handcrafted to your exact specifications. When you hire us, you deal directly with the master craftsman on the workshop floor, not a sales representative.
